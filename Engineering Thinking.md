## Core Engineering Mindset

1. **Logic Over Rote Memorization**
   - Do not blindly memorize terminal commands or complex code syntax. Instead, **understand the underlying logic and flow**. When you hit a problem, know where to look, verify the answer, and use it to get things done. *Remember the concepts and patterns; look up the details on the fly.*

2. **Understanding Tools vs. Syntax**
   - The tool we use to issue commands (e.g., Windows **PowerShell**, Linux **Bash**) is just a terminal interface. The core principles of tools like **Git** remain identical regardless of the environment. 
   - *Don't get trapped by the surface-level differences of the operating system; focus on the unifying patterns.*
